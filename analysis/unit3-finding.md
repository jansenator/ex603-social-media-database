# Finding for the dashboard team

The dashboard reported that about half of all reactions ended as spam removals. Leadership froze creator monetization on that figure. An established creator said the rate did not match what they see on the platform.

The reaction log has 200 reactions. 156 are still active. 10 are flagged for review and were not removed. 34 were removed, and 12 of those removals were for spam. Spam removals are 12 of 200 reactions, about 6 percent. The like totals stored on each post are a separate set of numbers from this reaction log, so a rate built from those post totals does not describe reactions.

Creator monetization should be restored. The query behind the dashboard left out the reactions that have no removal reason on file. Those are most of the log, so the share that remained looked much worse than the full set. A second part of the query used a label before the database had finished computing it, so that part could not run. The 12 spam removals should stay removed. The 10 flagged reactions need a review. They are not a reason to keep the freeze.
